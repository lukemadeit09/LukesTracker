# Privacy Policy — Lukes Tracker

**Effective: July 2026**

Lukes Tracker does not collect any data. Period.

- **No accounts.** There is nothing to sign up for and no way to log in.
- **No servers.** The app makes zero network requests. There is no
  backend, no sync, no cloud.
- **No tracking.** No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising
  identifiers, no third-party SDKs that phone home.
- **Your data stays on your device.** Habits, goals, progress history,
  and settings are stored locally on your phone (in the app's private
  storage) and nowhere else. Deleting the app deletes the data.
- **Notifications are local.** Daily reminders and milestone alerts are
  scheduled on-device. No push-notification service or device token is
  ever used.
- **Export is yours.** The optional "Export data" feature hands your
  data to the iOS/Android share sheet as JSON. Where you send it is
  entirely up to you; the app never transmits it anywhere itself.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to sell, share, or
breach, and nothing to request deletion of — your device already holds
the only copy.
